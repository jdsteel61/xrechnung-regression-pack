## 1. What the Service is

The Release Regression Pack is a **one-off testing and regression aid** for
businesses working with the XRechnung e-invoicing format.

- You supply your own XRechnung **test** fixtures: up to **250** items,
  identified by opaque IDs. We accept **XML bytes only** — no archives — and
  each upload is byte-bounded at intake.
- We validate your fixtures with the **official KoSIT XRechnung validator
  1.6.3** against two pinned ruleset configurations (release-2025-07-10 and
  v2026-01-31), which include CEN EN 16931 rules, Schematron rules, UBL
  Invoice/CreditNote and CII Cross-Industry Invoice schemas, and Peppol
  assets, as bundled by the KoSIT and CEN distributions.
- You receive a **comparison report** in three formats: JSON, JUnit XML, and
  readable HTML.
- You may request **one rerun** of the same comparison within seven days of
  your order.
